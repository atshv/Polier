### Builder's open question: add_task keeps its own atomic writer

Acceptable for now. The writer is a temp file plus `os.replace` under the same `board.json.lock` that `core/state.py` uses, so the two cannot interleave or tear the file; it is a duplicated write helper, not a second implementation of a rule. `core/state.py` is outside M04's scope.
