# Knowledge and intake

The knowledge section brings every document of the project and this handbook into one place, and intake turns ideas into planned work.

## Pages

Pages shows two kinds of documents:

- **Project documents** — `README`, `ARCHITECTURE`, `AGENTS`, `STATUS`, everything under `docs/`, task briefs, reviews and package READMEs. They are read-only here; agents edit them through the write guard.
- **This handbook** — guides and the API reference, shipped with the dashboard.

Use the search above the navigation, star pages to keep them under *Favorites*, follow *Referenced by* to the tasks and pages that link to a document, and open *Page history* to read an older revision from git.

## Intake

Ideas, issues and requests as committed proposals, triaged into tasks. See [Intake and templates](polier:intake-and-templates).
